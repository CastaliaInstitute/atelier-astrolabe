What crosses the threshold

The Spotify login stays on Spotify. Skype sends the resulting refresh credential only to the Astrolabe you select over Bluetooth; it does not send that credential to Castalia.

Playback commands require Spotify Premium and act on an active Spotify app or Spotify Connect speaker. Astrolabe is the remote control, not the audio endpoint.
