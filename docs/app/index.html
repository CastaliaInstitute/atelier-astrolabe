    Privacy & research

    Your reflection belongs to you.

    Reading ratings always teach your own LunaSay locally. Pseudonymous research sharing is a separate choice below.

    Help LunaSay learn what resonates

    Share only a selected mood and bounded scores, or a face, helpful/mixed/missed rating, reading date, time, and salted device identifier.

    The GitHub research export never includes your name, reading text or evidence, birth details, family data, journal or conversation text, ring biometrics, location, notes, or raw device identifier.

    Connect LunaSay to view research status.

    How withdrawal works

    Turning sharing off immediately stops new exports and deletes any unsent contribution from the device. It does not delete earlier contributions; LunaSay still needs a reviewed deletion-request process before research sharing can launch.

    Clear reading history

    Connect an updated LunaSay to erase its seven-day reading thread, cached daily packets, cached reading audio, and private resonance signal.

    Private by default · LunaSay is a reflective instrument, not a medical device.
