How it fits your day

No feed. No app grid. Astrolabe is made for quick check-ins and short conversations, so you stay present in real life.

1

Glance

Check time, moon phase, or your transit wheel in one quick look. The interface is intentionally simple and easy to read.

2

Ask

Hold the side button and ask aloud about your day, tonight's sky, or your chart. Castalia responds with a clear spoken answer.

3

Listen and continue

Tap the second button to replay the last response, then put Astrolabe away. It supports your routine without hijacking it.

Common questions

Do I need a Mynah phone?

No. Astrolabe connects to Castalia directly over Wi‑Fi. Mynah is optional, and deeper cross-device features are on the roadmap.

Is this a smartwatch?

Not in the typical sense. It is intentionally simpler: round display, two buttons, no app store, and no endless notification feed.

When does it ship?

Early access units ship in waves through 2026. We confirm your expected window by email after you request a spot.

For builders and developers

Hardware & repo

Reference board: Waveshare ESP32-S3 Touch AMOLED 1.75C class (466×466 round AMOLED). Firmware and build instructions: github.com/CastaliaInstitute/astrolabe.

Chart accuracy in early firmware

V0 uses on-device approximations today; server-side ephemeris is in progress. Founders receive updates as accuracy improves — no extra hardware required.
