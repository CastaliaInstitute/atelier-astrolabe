Common architecture

Voice. Memory. Presence.

All Astrolabe devices share a unified round module and magnetic dock integration. The shell changes the ritual; the core hardware and firmware lineage stay familiar.

Module

1.75-inch round AMOLED, USB-C charging and data, Wi-Fi, BLE, and offline-capable firmware.

Sensors

Temperature, heart-rate/PPG, respiration, accelerometer, ambient light, sound, and pressure pathways.

Docking

Magnetic alignment, secure hold, charging display, and keyed orientation for accessory shells.

Interface

Touch, side buttons, speaker, microphone, and I2C expansion for mount and electrode accessories.
