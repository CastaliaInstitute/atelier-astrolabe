How inquiry moves

No feed. No app grid. Astrolabe works like a physical prompt: glance when a question is forming, swipe to change the frame, ask only when you are ready to hear an answer.

1

Glance

Wake the round face for time, moon phase, weather, presence, music, or symbol. The first answer is visual: enough to orient, not enough to overwhelm.

2

Ask

Hold the side button and ask aloud. Castalia answers through the faculty and context you allow, turning a device interaction into a short dialogue.

3

Swipe

Swipe left or right to change the question. Swipe up or down when the active face has its own inner motion: tone, key, mode, card, or next layer.

Common questions

Do I need a Mynah phone?

No. Astrolabe talks to Castalia over Wi‑Fi on its own. Mynah is the reference companion in the same household — closer integration is on the roadmap.

Is this a smartwatch?

It’s closer to a pocket instrument: round display, two buttons, no app store, no notifications feed. You buy it for time, sky, and short voice turns with Castalia.

Is a Castalia Membership required?

No. Astrolabe should remain a complete contemplative instrument without a subscription. Membership unlocks the living academy layer: premium faculty, cloud memory, Dialogic conversations, advanced astrology, voice generation, community, lectures, and seasonal rituals.

When does it ship?
