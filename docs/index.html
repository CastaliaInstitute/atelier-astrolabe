How you use it

No feed. No app grid. The interaction is closer to an instrument: glance, swipe the round face, ask when a question is ready.

1

Glance

Wake the round face for time, moon phase, weather, presence, music, or symbolic inquiry — made for a two-second look, not a session.

2

Ask

Hold the side button and ask aloud. The module records, thinks, and answers through Castalia’s voice service using the context you allow.

3

Swipe

Swipe left or right to move between faces. Swipe up or down only when the active face has its own gesture, like changing tone, key, mode, or card.

Common questions

Do I need a Mynah phone?

No. Astrolabe talks to Castalia over Wi‑Fi on its own. Mynah is the reference companion in the same household — closer integration is on the roadmap.

Is this a smartwatch?

It’s closer to a pocket instrument: round display, two buttons, no app store, no notifications feed. You buy it for time, sky, and short voice turns with Castalia.

Is a Castalia Membership required?

No. Astrolabe should remain a complete contemplative instrument without a subscription. Membership unlocks the living academy layer: premium faculty, cloud memory, Dialogic conversations, advanced astrology, voice generation, community, lectures, and seasonal rituals.
