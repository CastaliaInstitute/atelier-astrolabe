# LunaSay production shot list

This is the real-product list for the Kickstarter and launch library. Use the
actual 1.75-inch circular device and the thin, rounded aluminum Type-C dock.
Generated social treatments are inspiration only; do not represent them as
product photography.

## Before the shoot

- [ ] Clean the black glass, aluminum shell, dock, and USB-C connector; bring a microfiber cloth.
- [ ] Prepare a dark Moon face, astrology face, transits, synastry, tarot, journal waveform, and settings face on working firmware.
- [ ] Charge and label two identical devices: one hero device and one backup.
- [ ] Confirm that the dock is the thin aluminum Type-C model and that the device seats cleanly on the connector.
- [ ] Pack a black linen, warm wood, pale stone, and neutral-gray surface; avoid props that make it look occult or steampunk.
- [ ] Record the exact firmware build, face, and time used in every final shot.

## Still photography

- [ ] **Hero, three-quarter:** device seated on the aluminum Type-C dock, Moon face lit, warm evening table, vertical and landscape crops.
- [ ] **True-scale pair:** device beside a hand, a coin, or an iPhone USB-C plug for honest scale; keep the comparison secondary.
- [ ] **Dock detail:** low-angle macro of the thin aluminum slab, rounded corners, and central Type-C connector before the device is seated.
- [ ] **Connection detail:** device being lowered onto the connector; a second frame fully seated.
- [ ] **Face set:** square and vertical close-ups of Moon, Astrology, Transits, Synastry, Tarot, Journal, Conversation, and Settings.
- [ ] **Material set:** black glass reflection, aluminum rim, aluminum dock edge, and connector macro—each in soft daylight.
- [ ] **Nightstand reality:** Moon face in a lived-in but uncluttered bedroom; enough context to feel human, not staged.
- [ ] **Desk reality:** Journal or Conversation face beside a notebook; no fake transcription claim on screen.
- [ ] **Settings proof:** battery, Wi-Fi, BLE, and connection controls visible on the actual device.
- [ ] **Packaging / scale:** final device, dock, USB-C cable, and packaging only after these are final production objects.

## Short video captures

- [ ] **Hero loop (8–10 s):** device approaches the dock, seats on USB-C, Moon face wakes, hold for two seconds.
- [ ] **Face tour (20–30 s):** one clean physical swipe per LunaSay face; capture the display without jump cuts between face changes.
- [ ] **Moon moment (10–12 s):** a dark room, one tap or wake, then a slow move toward the Moon face.
- [ ] **Voice question (15–20 s):** natural question, visible listening state, response begins, then one short audible sentence. Record sync sound and a clean room tone pass.
- [ ] **Journal (12–15 s):** intentional hold-to-speak gesture, live waveform, release, then the resulting private transcript on the authorized surface.
- [ ] **Synastry (12–15 s):** two people place the device between them; show the relationship-weather face without claiming prediction or diagnosis.
- [ ] **Settings / connection (10–15 s):** dock connection, open settings, show Wi-Fi or Bluetooth status; no iPhone app claim.
- [ ] **Charging proof (8–10 s):** close detail of the USB-C dock connection and charging state.
- [ ] **Silent product b-roll:** 24 fps slow push, 60 fps hand movement, and at least five seconds of stable clean plates for every setup.

## Capture rules

- [ ] Shoot 4K master files; frame both 16:9 and 9:16 intentionally.
- [ ] Lock exposure and white balance when filming the display to prevent flicker or blown-out whites.
- [ ] Capture a clean screen recording or direct display capture for every filmed face as a backup insert.
- [ ] Record exact spoken prompts and response timing; retain only takes that the current firmware can reproduce.
- [ ] Do not use generated product renders, fabricated UI, or unimplemented claims in Kickstarter or technical demonstration footage.
