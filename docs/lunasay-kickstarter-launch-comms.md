## Message discipline

Every communication should reinforce three truths:

1. LunaSay is a working physical prototype, not a rendering.
2. It listens during an explicit hold-to-talk gesture, not ambiently.
3. Astrology, synastry, tarot, and the Alethiometer offer symbolic reflection,
   not deterministic, medical, legal, financial, or relationship advice.

Use **LunaSay** as the product name and **pocket astrolabe** as the memorable
category. Say **cloud voice** when describing STT–LLM–TTS. Never say “offline
AI,” “always knows,” “scientifically predicts,” “replaces your phone,” or
“unlimited AI.”

## Comment-response guide

### “Is this AI astrology?”

It combines on-device visual faces with optional cloud speech and language
interpretation. We present astrology, synastry, tarot, and the Alethiometer as
symbolic reflection, not scientific prediction or objective advice.

### “Is it recording all the time?”

No. The campaign prototype uses an explicit hold-to-talk interaction. We will
publish the final data flow and retention behavior before launch.

### “Why does it need the cloud?”

The current spoken path uses cloud transcription, language interpretation, and
speech synthesis. We will show exactly what remains local, what needs Wi-Fi,
the included allowance, and the unavailable-service behavior.

### “Why not just use a phone app?”

LunaSay is intentionally a physical glance and question ritual. It does less
than a phone and asks for less attention. Whether that difference is valuable
is exactly what the campaign is testing.

### “When will it ship?”

We will publish a delivery window only after manufacturing, compliance, packed
freight, pilot, and fulfillment lead times are quoted. The campaign timeline
will include those stages and an explicit buffer.

### “Can you add another face/stretch goal?”

We are keeping the launch scope bounded. New hardware and untested faces are
not stretch goals; additional funding first strengthens testing, support
inventory, documentation, and delivery confidence.
